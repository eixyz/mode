The Embassy of the Republic of Mozambique in Germany invites all Mozambicans living in Germany to a meeting with H.E. Maria de Fátima Simão Manso, Secretary of State for Foreign Affairs and Mozambican Communities Abroad.

Date: Saturday, 21 November 2026

Venue: Allee der Kosmonauten 32, 12681 Berlin

3:00 pm – Meeting with leaders of Mozambican community associations and organisations in Germany

4:00 pm – Meeting with the entire Mozambican community in Germany

The meetings will be followed by a reception. We look forward to seeing you!
